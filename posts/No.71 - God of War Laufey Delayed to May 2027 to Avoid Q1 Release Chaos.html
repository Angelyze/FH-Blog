God of War Laufey drops its February 2027 launch for a May 20, 2027 release date, according to a report on Santa Monica Studio's schedule shift.

Editorial note: This article is based on press reporting and has not been confirmed by the developer or publisher.

Sony Santa Monica has reportedly pushed back Faye's standalone title, clearing out of a stacked early 2027 release window.

Look, I know we usually live and breathe the wasteland here at the Hub, but when a heavy hitter like Santa Monica Studio moves a release date, the whole industry feels the tremor. According to a report from Wccftech, God of War Laufey is stepping out of its original February 16, 2027, launch slot. The new target is reportedly May 20, 2027. It’s a three-month bump that moves Faye’s standalone adventure out of a Q1 window that’s looking increasingly like a demolition derby for AAA titles. While the news is still filtering through official channels, the reported statement from the studio suggests they're prioritizing the final coat of paint over hitting a winter deadline.

Key facts

  • Reported by Wccftech; not yet confirmed by the developer or publisher.
  • Reported by Wccftech; the delay moves the game from February 16 to May 20, 2027.
  • Santa Monica Studio reportedly cited the need for extra development time to meet fan expectations.
  • The shift avoids a crowded Q1 2027 window currently being reshaped by the 'GTA VI fallout.'
  • The project focuses on Laufey (Faye) and her journey through the 'Everywhen,' first revealed in June.

Three Extra Months for Santa Monica Studio

According to the report from Wccftech, Santa Monica Studio isn't interested in rushing Faye's big moment. The studio reportedly issued a statement explaining that since the game's reveal back in June, the level of fan excitement has been massive. They noted, 'We don't take for granted the trust placed in the studio to make an extraordinary experience worthy of the passion and dedication of our fans.' That’s developer-speak for 'we need more time to get the Everywhen looking right.' This three-month extension to May 20, 2027, gives the team the breathing room to iron out the technical kinks that usually plague massive, high-fidelity action titles. For a studio known for technical excellence, taking an extra ninety days to ensure the combat feels as weighty as Kratos’s original run is a move most of us can get behind, even if the wait stings a bit.

Ditching the Crowded Q1 2027 Calendar

Beyond just internal polish, moving out of February is a tactical retreat that makes a lot of sense. The report points out that early 2027 is shaping up to be an absolute gauntlet for AAA releases. Much of this is being attributed to the 'GTA VI fallout'—the industry-wide scheduling shifts that happen whenever a Rockstar juggernaut looms on the horizon. When a game that big moves, every other publisher starts sweating over their own launch windows. By sliding into late May, Sony gives Laufey a chance to dominate the conversation in the late spring rather than fighting for oxygen in a February window that’s already looking overcrowded. It’s better to be the biggest game in May than just another face in the crowd during a chaotic Q1.

What We Know About Faye's Journey

Details on God of War Laufey are still being kept close to the chest, but we know the project centers on Laufey—better known to most of us as Faye, Atreus’s mother and Kratos’s late wife. Since the 2018 soft reboot, she’s been this towering lore figure who we only ever saw as a shroud or heard about through stories. This standalone title is set to explore her journey through the 'Everywhen,' a concept that has lore hunters doing overtime. Santa Monica Studio’s reported update indicates they want to ensure her leading role gets the care it deserves. Giving a character this pivotal her own game is a big swing, and rushing it out the door just to hit a quarterly earnings target would be a disservice to a character who basically set the entire modern God of War plot in motion.

Waiting on Official Publisher Word

It is worth keeping in mind that while Wccftech has shared the studio's message regarding the delay, Sony hasn't hit the 'send' button on a global press release just yet. If you check the digital store pages right now, you might still see the old February date staring back at you. Until the official social accounts and the PlayStation Blog lock in the May 20th date, there’s always a slim chance of further movement. However, a three-month delay is a very specific and realistic window for a project of this scale. It’s long enough to fix significant bugs but short enough that the marketing momentum from the June reveal won't completely evaporate. We’ll be watching the official feeds to see when the change becomes 'official-official.'

Why fans are talking about this: Sliding out of a chaotic Q1 window gives high-profile titles like Laufey the breathing room they actually need to launch without being buried by the competition.

Official confirmation from Sony and Santa Monica Studio is still pending on the main public feeds, so don't go crossing out your February calendars in permanent marker just yet. That said, the logic behind the move is sound. Between the technical demands of the 'Everywhen' and the messy state of the 2027 release schedule, May feels like a much safer bet for a game of this caliber. We'll keep an eye on the official channels as May 20, 2027, approaches to see if this date sticks.
