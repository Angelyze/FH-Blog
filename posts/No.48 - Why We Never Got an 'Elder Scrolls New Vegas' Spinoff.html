Former Bethesda dev Kurt Kuhlmann claims Todd Howard’s protective nature over the IP is why The Elder Scrolls never got a New Vegas-style spinoff.

Editorial note: This article is based on press reporting and has not been confirmed by the developer or publisher.

A long-time Bethesda veteran sheds light on why the studio hasn't let outside teams touch their fantasy crown jewel.

For years, we've wondered why Bethesda hasn't given the Elder Scrolls the 'New Vegas' treatment by handing the keys to another studio for a one-off project. According to a new report from IGN, former Bethesda designer Kurt Kuhlmann says the answer comes down to Todd Howard being 'very protective' of the franchise. While these claims offer a rare look behind the curtain, it's important to note that Bethesda and Microsoft haven't officially confirmed these internal dynamics.

Key facts

  • Reported by IGN; not yet confirmed by the developer or publisher.
  • Former lead designer Kurt Kuhlmann claims Todd Howard is 'very protective' of the Elder Scrolls IP.
  • The 'who makes it better' debate surrounding Fallout: New Vegas reportedly made Bethesda cautious about repeating the experiment.
  • The Elder Scrolls Online is credited with 'taking the heat off' the need for a single-player spinoff.
  • The Elder Scrolls 6 remains years away, though Xbox leadership recently saw a demo of the game.

The Shadow of New Vegas

It’s no secret that Fallout: New Vegas is a bit of a touchy subject in the community, often sparking endless debates about whether Obsidian or Bethesda 'does it better.' According to Kuhlmann, who spent 20 years at Bethesda, that exact perception is why we haven't seen a similar spinoff for Tamriel. He told FRVR that the New Vegas situation created a specific narrative that Todd Howard likely didn't want to replicate. When fans start arguing that a third-party studio is better at handling your own IP than you are, it creates a weird internal pressure that Bethesda seems keen to avoid.

Todd Howard's Protective Streak

Kuhlmann described Howard as being particularly guarded when it comes to the Elder Scrolls. While Fallout has been allowed to branch out into different hands and even a high-profile TV show, the fantasy series remains the studio's primary baby. The report suggests that Howard’s protective stance isn't just about quality control, but about maintaining the specific identity of the franchise. If you've ever felt like Bethesda treats Elder Scrolls as the more 'sacred' of their two big pillars, Kuhlmann’s comments pretty much back that up.

ESO as the Pressure Valve

One reason the demand for a spinoff hasn't forced Bethesda's hand is the success of The Elder Scrolls Online. Kuhlmann noted that ZeniMax Online Studios' MMO 'took the heat off' the idea of needing another team to fill the gaps between mainline releases. For a huge portion of the player base, ESO is their primary way to experience the world, and it provides a constant stream of content that a single-player spinoff would only provide once. It’s a different beast than a traditional RPG, but for the suits at ZeniMax, it seemingly checked the box for 'doing something with the IP.'

The Long Road to TES 6

While we wait for the next mainline entry, which was announced way back in 2018, the franchise is still moving forward behind the scenes. Xbox CEO Asha Sharma recently mentioned seeing a demo for the new game, praising its scale, but the reality is that we’re still years away from actually playing it. Without a spinoff to bridge the gap, fans are left with the choice of sticking with ESO or modding Skyrim for the thousandth time. It’s a long wait, but if Kuhlmann is right, Bethesda would rather we wait than risk another 'who did it better' comparison with an outside studio.

Why fans are talking about this: The 'New Vegas' comparison is a double-edged sword that Bethesda seems determined to avoid repeating with their other flagship franchise.

It’s fascinating to hear that the legacy of New Vegas might be the very thing keeping us from seeing a different studio’s take on Tamriel. We’ll have to wait for official word from Bethesda to see if their stance on spinoffs ever shifts, but for now, it looks like the mainline path is the only one they're interested in walking.
