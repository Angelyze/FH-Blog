Grab 12-packs of Fallout-themed AriZona Iced Tea on sale for Black Friday, including the new Apocalyptic Orange flavor featuring show character art.

Amazon's Black Friday discount drops prices on AriZona's 12-can Fallout cases, including the new Apocalyptic Orange flavor tied to the TV show.

If you’re like me, your desk is probably already littered with half-empty cans and NCR memorabilia. If you like stocking up on cheap caffeine while roaming the wastes, Amazon just knocked down the price on AriZona's official Fallout iced tea collection for Black Friday (via IGN). It’s a nice break because, let’s be honest, the markup on licensed drinks usually hurts. This deal applies to 12-can cases of all four themed flavors, bringing the price of these collectible cans a bit closer to AriZona's famous 99-cent standard. That includes the lineup's newest addition: Apocalyptic Orange.

Key facts

  • Confirmed via official Bethesda communications; coverage from IGN.
  • A 15% discount applies to 12-can cases of Fallout-themed AriZona Iced Tea on Amazon for Black Friday.
  • The lineup features four flavors: Atomic Red Apple, Wastelander Sweet Peach, Power Pomegranate, and the new Apocalyptic Orange.
  • Case packaging features playing card artwork of TV show characters including Lucy, The Ghoul, Maximus, and Dogmeat.
  • AriZona founder Don Vultaggio maintains a 99-cent MSRP for standard cans, while licensed Fallout packs sit near $2 per 15oz can before discounts.

Four Flavors, One Brand-New Brew

The collaboration brings three Vault-Tec twists on classic AriZona recipes alongside one totally new creation. You've got Atomic Red Apple, Wastelander Sweet Peach, and Power Pomegranate taking on familiar AriZona roots, plus Apocalyptic Orange making its debut specifically for this crossover. They come packaged in 15oz cans sold strictly in 12-packs, so you can't just grab a single can off a gas station shelf for a buck. If you aren't sure which flavor to commit to, the Black Friday deal also extends to AriZona's standard bulk variety packs, which is a safer bet if you're worried about whether 'Apocalyptic Orange' actually tastes like the end of the world or just a really good citrus blend.

Show Art and Character Crossovers

What makes these cans stand out on a shelf isn't just the tea inside, but the custom playing card illustrations covering the packaging. Each flavor pairs up with characters from Amazon's live-action Fallout series. Lucy gets the Red Apple flavor, while The Ghoul takes Wastelander Sweet Peach. If you're watching your sugar intake, the low-cal variants feature Dogmeat and pre-war Cooper Howard. Meanwhile, Maximus pulls double duty: his un-armored self is featured on the new Apocalyptic Orange box, while his suit of Power Armor adorns the Power Pomegranate packs. It’s a cool touch for collectors, even if we’re all just going to recycle the cans eventually—or stack them into a pyramid like a proper vault dweller.

The Price Tag Reality Check

AriZona founder Don Vultaggio is famous for fiercely protecting the brand's standard 99-cent MSRP on normal cans, but licensed crossovers operate under a different set of rules. The Fallout 12-packs normally work out to around two bucks a can, which is a steep jump for tea. While Amazon's Black Friday sale isn't a massive price crash—it’s a 15% discount that knocks off a couple of dollars per case—it's still a welcome discount if you were planning to buy a case anyway. It gets these collector-style cans just a little bit closer to that classic dollar price point we’ve come to expect from the brand.

A Long Wait for New Fallout Games

With Season 2 of the TV show wrapped earlier this year and Season 3 not expected on Prime Video until around 2027, tie-in merchandise like this tea is carrying a lot of the brand presence right now. On the gaming front, Bethesda Game Studios has turned its attention back to 'Sentinel'—the confirmed name for the next Elder Scrolls entry. Since Bethesda already confirmed Fallout 5 won't arrive until after Sentinel, and that game is still years away, drinking iced tea and replaying New Vegas might be our primary Fallout activity for quite a while. It’s a long drought, but at least we’ll be hydrated while we wait for the next big update.

Why fans are talking about this: It's not a massive price crash, but getting custom Fallout tea cans closer to standard AriZona pricing makes stocking the fridge easier.

While we are still years away from Fallout 5 or even the third season of the TV show, little official crossovers like this give fans something fun to grab. Grab a case while the Black Friday pricing is active on Amazon if you want to stock your fridge or display the artwork. Just don't expect it to actually restore your HP in real life.
