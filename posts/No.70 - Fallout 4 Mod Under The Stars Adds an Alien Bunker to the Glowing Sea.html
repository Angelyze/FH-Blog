The Commonwealth Story Expansion Project has released Under The Stars, a new Fallout 4 quest mod uncovering a secret alien-tinged bunker in the Glowing Sea.

Editorial note: This article is based on press reporting and has not been confirmed by the developer or publisher.

As reported by Rock Paper Shotgun, CSEP's fresh Glowing Sea dungeon crawl was built for the underground-themed Fallout Flood modding jam.

If you ever needed an excuse to pull a hazmat suit out of storage and trudge back into the Glowing Sea, the Commonwealth modding scene just delivered one. According to a report by Rock Paper Shotgun, the crew over at the Commonwealth Story Expansion Project (CSEP) has launched a brand-new Fallout 4 quest mod titled Under The Stars. Fresh off adding an entire custom town to the game just last month, the modding team has turned its sights south toward Boston's nastiest, most radioactive expanse with a sunken bunker packed with mystery.

Key facts

  • Reported by Rock Paper Shotgun; not yet confirmed by the developer or publisher.
  • Reported by Rock Paper Shotgun; fan-made mod release not officially affiliated with Bethesda.
  • Created by the Commonwealth Story Expansion Project (CSEP) as part of the Fallout Flood mod jam.
  • Takes players into a sunken Glowing Sea bunker featuring voiced holotapes, puzzles, and new gear.
  • Visuals tease an Area 51 aesthetic with hints of extra-terrestrial oddities and strange experiments.
  • Requires the base Fallout 4 game alongside both the Automatron and Vault-Tec Workshop DLCs.

A Sunken Bunker in the Commonwealth's Worst Weather

The Glowing Sea is already the most hostile stretch of terrain in post-war Massachusetts, where constant rad storms and aggressive local fauna make casual sightseeing impossible. Under The Stars turns that radioactive desolation into the staging ground for a classic dungeon delve. According to the CSEP team's official description, the harsh radioactive winds churning across the sea have steadily worn away the earth, uncovering a deep underground facility filled with secrets and horrors. As Rock Paper Shotgun details, players who take on the quest aren't just walking into an empty shoot-em-up corridor. The subterranean bunker is constructed as a proper investigative crawl, complete with voiced holotapes left behind to piece together what occurred before the blast doors sealed shut. You'll also encounter environmental puzzles to solve while working your way through the complex, with new gear waiting as a reward to make surviving the rads and whatever is prowling the dark corridors genuinely worth the ammo.

Area 51 Vibes and Cryptic Oddities

While the modders have kept the exact story twists guarded to let players experience them firsthand, the imagery featured in the report leans heavily into classic retro-futuristic sci-fi paranoia. The screenshots suggest an underground hideaway built with distinct Area 51 flavors—housing cryptic oddities, bizarre laboratory setups, and tantalizing hints of alien encounters. For anyone who follows Commonwealth modding, CSEP having their hands on this is a strong mark of quality. The team has built an impressive catalog of beloved Fallout 4 narrative mods tackling everything from murderous wasteland cults and gritty detective work to full-scale underground faction wars and heavy Brotherhood of Steel drama. While Under The Stars is a more concentrated dungeon dive rather than a sprawling multi-hour regional overhaul like the town project they dropped last month, their background indicates a tightly polished adventure designed to fit naturally right into the Commonwealth's map.

Born from the Fallout Flood Mod Jam

Under The Stars wasn't cooked up in a vacuum; it came to life through Fallout Flood, a community-hosted modding jam that challenged creators across the scene to design content around a shared underground theme. Mod jams like this are always a blast because they push people to ship strange, inventive ideas on a deadline, and this particular gathering produced a surprisingly eclectic lineup of wasteland creations. Alongside CSEP's bunker mystery, Rock Paper Shotgun highlighted several other offbeat gems from the jam. Those include Stranded - The Oasis, another dedicated Fallout 4 quest mod; an underground dungeon infested with funky rats designated as the S'Laven; and a bizarre mechanical enemy that you can literally slap onto your character's head and wear as a robot hat. The event even crossed state borders into the Mojave, producing a Fallout: New Vegas quest mod where the Courier explores an abandoned NCR railway system.

What You Need in Your Load Order Before Heading In

If you are ready to boot up your mod manager and track down this facility, you will want to make sure your game files are squared away first. Rock Paper Shotgun notes that Under The Stars requires you to own both the Automatron DLC and the Vault-Tec Workshop DLC to function without missing assets or throwing errors. Once you have confirmed those plugins are active in your load order, pack plenty of RadAway, step into your lead-lined armor, and set your compass south toward the storm.
