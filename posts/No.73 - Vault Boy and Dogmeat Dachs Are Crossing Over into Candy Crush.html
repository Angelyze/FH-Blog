Vault Boy and Dogmeat armor arrive in Candy Crush Saga for Xbox's 25th anniversary, along with orchestral, candified Fallout music tracks.

Bethesda and King have teamed up to celebrate Xbox's 25th anniversary with four weeks of sweet Fallout pins and reworked wasteland music.

If you had "Vault Boy showing up in Candy Crush Saga" on your 2026 Xbox anniversary bingo card, go ahead and collect your prize. I certainly didn't expect to see the Wasteland's favorite mascot sitting next to colorful jelly beans, but here we are. Xbox Wire officially confirmed that King's hit mobile puzzle game is hosting a four-week "Game On!" season celebrating 25 years of Xbox, bringing Fallout, Halo, DOOM, and more into the Candy Kingdom. This isn't just a quick logo swap, either; it’s a full-on aesthetic takeover that includes collectible pins and some surprisingly high-effort orchestral takes on iconic Fallout music.

Key facts

  • Confirmed via official Bethesda communications; coverage from Xbox Wire.
  • King and Bethesda teamed up for a 4-week "Game On!" season in Candy Crush Saga celebrating Xbox's 25th anniversary.
  • Fallout pins include Vault Boy, Dachs wearing Dogmeat's leather armor, and Bubblegum Troll dressed in a Vault Suit.
  • Iconic Fallout music was re-recorded with a full orchestra, the Bulgarian Voices Berlin choir, and an accordion.
  • The reworked six-track soundtrack (covering Fallout, Halo, DOOM, and more) will be released on music streaming platforms.
  • The event also features Halo's Master Chief, DOOM's Cacodemon, and characters from Sea of Thieves and Banjo-Kazooie.

Vault Suits and Dogmeat Armor in the Candy Kingdom

The core of the event is an Xbox 25 pin collection earned by completing levels during the month-long season. For Fallout fans, that means unlocking Vault Boy, seeing Dachs dressed up in Dogmeat's leather armor, and watching Bubblegum Troll squeeze into a Vault Suit. It’s a bit surreal to see the gritty aesthetic of the Mojave or the Commonwealth filtered through a neon-sugar lens, but it works better than you’d think. Daniel Lee, Associate Art Director at Bethesda Game Studios, joked about the crossover fit: "Sugar Bombs, Fancy Lad Snack Cakes, Radioactive Gumdrops—the Wasteland is certainly no stranger to sweet treats." He noted that working with King to mash up the two very different visual styles was a real pleasure for the studio team, specifically pointing out that seeing Dachs in Dogmeat's gear was a personal highlight.

Candifying the Sound of the Wasteland

The visual crossover is cute, but the audio treatment is where this gets genuinely interesting for those of us who appreciate the series' atmosphere. Composer Federico Truzzi worked with King’s Head of Audio Dominique Devoucoux and Audio Lead Sebastian Aav to re-arrange six iconic themes—including Fallout, Halo, DOOM, and Banjo-Kazooie—into a playful, distinct Candy Crush style. These aren't just cheap MIDI covers. The recordings brought together a full orchestra and the Bulgarian Voices Berlin choir. They even added an accordion to the mix to give it that specific whimsical flair. If you want to listen to these tracks without actually matching digital candies, all six are being made available across streaming platforms and participating studio channels. Hearing the Fallout theme backed by a world-class choir and an accordion is the kind of weird, high-quality fan service I can get behind.

Sharing the Spotlight with Xbox Royalty

Fallout isn't the only IP getting the sugary makeover during the 25th anniversary run. The "Game On!" season is a massive nostalgia mashup that includes Halo, Sea of Thieves, Forza, DOOM, Banjo-Kazooie, Crash Bandicoot, and Spyro. Halo Franchise Manager Corrine Robinson highlighted Tiffi wearing pink Mjolnir armor alongside Dachs as a Grunt, while Rare brought Banjo, Kazooie, and even Pendragon into the mix. You'll also spot Master Chief’s helmet, DOOM’s Cacodemon, and Crash Bandicoot’s Dr. Neo Cortex as collectible pins. It’s a massive effort designed to reach Candy Crush's massive casual player base while giving long-time console players a reason to chuckle at their favorite characters looking a little more 'edible' than usual.

The Art of the Mashup

Bringing these worlds together meant figuring out which details to keep and which to play with. For Rare, the collaboration was about more than just a cameo. Adam Park, Head of Brand and Licensing at Rare, mentioned that the universal appeal of Candy Crush was a great way to introduce characters like Banjo and Kazooie to a whole new audience. The teams behind these franchises worked closely with King on the designs to ensure that even when a character is 'candified,' they remain unmistakably themselves. Whether it’s the specific curve of a Spartan helmet or the iconic blue and yellow of a Vault Suit, the goal was to keep the soul of the IP intact. King even released a behind-the-scenes video featuring their audio leads discussing how they balanced the 'playful and unexpected' nature of the mobile game with the legendary status of these soundtracks.

Why fans are talking about this: Seeing Dachs in Dogmeat gear is weirdly charming, but getting an orchestral accordion remix of Fallout music on Spotify is the real prize here.

The Game On! season is live right now in Candy Crush Saga for a limited four-week run. Exact streaming drop dates for the re-recorded soundtrack tracks haven't been listed yet, so keep an eye on official studio channels if you just want the music without grinding through hundreds of puzzle levels. It's a strange crossover, sure, but it's a fun way to acknowledge how far these franchises have come over the last 25 years.
