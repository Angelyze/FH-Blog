Grab your free Xbox 25th Anniversary profile badge, check out community-made themes, and see if the global FanFest tour is stopping near you.

Xbox is celebrating 25 years of gaming with free community-created profile gear, a massive global tour, and curated Game Pass collections.

Xbox is officially kicking off its 25th-anniversary celebrations ahead of the big milestone on November 15, and they have laid out a roadmap of fan events and free digital goodies. According to the latest update from Xbox Wire, we are looking at everything from community-designed console themes to a global FanFest tour. It has been a long road since the original 'Duke' controller days, and while the brand has changed a lot, it is nice to see them leaning into the history that brought us here. If you have been looking for an excuse to refresh your dashboard or see some fellow gamers in person, this is your heads-up.

Key facts

  • Confirmed via official Bethesda communications; coverage from Xbox Wire.
  • A free Xbox 25th Anniversary profile badge is available now through the end of 2026 just by signing in.
  • Xbox partnered with community creators Klobrille and Ben Kenobi for free gamerpics, backgrounds, and dynamic themes.
  • A global FanFest Tour is hitting cities like London, Tokyo, Mexico City, Toronto, and Sydney via ticket sweepstakes.
  • Curated Game Pass collections are live, highlighting generation-defining titles including Fallout 4 and Fable Anniversary.

Free Community-Created Dashboard Gear

Xbox is handing out free customization items designed by community creators Klobrille and Ben Kenobi. It is a rare move to see a platform holder give actual community creators the spotlight for official dashboard themes, and it is a welcome change from standard corporate marketing. They have put together a set of gamerpics, backgrounds, and a dynamic theme for Xbox consoles that you can grab right now. To set them up on your console, head to Settings, then Profile, and select Customize Profile. If you are looking for the dynamic background, you will find it under Settings > General > Personalization > My background. You can also update your gamerpic and theme through the Xbox app on PC by selecting the pencil icon over your current profile image. If you make something cool with these assets, they are asking people to share them using the #XBOX25 hashtag.

The Game Pass Nostalgia Trip

To mark the occasion, Xbox is curating special Game Pass collections that highlight the biggest games from each console generation. They are grouping together classics from the original Xbox all the way up to the Series X|S. Among the highlighted titles are Fable Anniversary, Halo: The Master Chief Collection, Psychonauts 2, and, of course, Fallout 4. For those of us who live in the Commonwealth, seeing Fallout 4 listed alongside the platform's 'generation-defining' titles feels right, especially after the recent next-gen update. It is a solid excuse to finally start that new playthrough you have been putting off, or to finally check out Fable if you missed the Peter Molyneux era of hype and broken promises.

FanFest Hits the Road Globally

If you prefer real-world gatherings, the Xbox FanFest Tour is going global. These are meant to be 'fan-first' events focused on the local community rather than just a corporate press release. After starting in Los Angeles and heading to gamescom in Cologne, Xbox announced the rest of the tour stops. They are hitting Tokyo on September 17 during the Tokyo Game Show, London on September 26, Mexico City and Sydney on October 17, and Toronto on November 6. There is also a Seattle stop planned, though the specific dates and venue details for that one are still being kept under wraps for now. It is a pretty aggressive schedule for the next few months, covering a lot of ground for fans who want to meet up in person.

How to Get Tickets and the Anniversary Badge

You cannot just show up to these FanFest events; tickets are being distributed through city-specific sweepstakes. If you want a shot at attending, you will need to register on the official Xbox FanFest page. It is a bit of a lottery, but worth a shot if you live near one of the tour stops and want to hang out with the local community. For those who cannot make it to a physical event, the 25th Anniversary profile badge is much easier to get. You will receive it automatically just by logging into your account on a console, PC, or the mobile app before the end of 2026. It is a small digital trinket, but it is a nice way to mark how long you have been part of the ecosystem.

Why fans are talking about this: It is rare to see a platform holder give actual community creators the spotlight for official dashboard themes, and it is a welcome change from standard corporate marketing.

While we still do not have the exact dates for the Seattle stop of the FanFest tour, the rest of the schedule is locked in. It is good to see creators like Klobrille and Ben Kenobi getting official recognition on the dashboard—it makes the whole celebration feel a little more human and a little less like a corporate mandate. Make sure to claim your profile badge before you forget, and keep an eye out for those local ticket sweepstakes if you are in one of the tour cities.
