Bethesda revealed the Fallout 76 World Pets update arriving December 1, letting players tame, name, level up, and command dogs, cats, and Deathclaws.

Bethesda has released a new overview trailer detailing pet customization, combat commands, and perk progression coming to Appalachia this December.

Bethesda has officially unveiled the World Pets overview trailer via Steam, locking in a release date of December 1 for full companion pets in Fallout 76. If you've spent any real time wandering Appalachia over the past six years, you know the community has been asking for actual, reliable animal companions practically since launch day. Up until now, the closest thing we had was the notoriously fussy C.A.M.P. creature taming system, where your pet would stay stuck at your base and could easily get picked off when you weren't looking. This new update changes that completely. Whether you want a faithful dog trotting beside you through the Forest, a quiet house cat watching your back, or a full-blown Deathclaw tearing through Scorched, pets are finally becoming active traveling companions with deep progression mechanics.

Key facts

  • Confirmed via official Bethesda communications; coverage from Steam — New Vegas.
  • The World Pets update officially launches for Fallout 76 on December 1.
  • Pet options range from faithful domestic Dogs and Cats to wilder picks like Radhogs and Deathclaws.
  • Companions feature custom naming, visual customization options, and an active leveling progression system.
  • Tactical command options allow players to direct their pets to attack, defend, or follow.
  • Leveling pets unlocks unique perks that integrate directly with specific player builds and playstyles.

More Than Just CAMP Decorations

For years, having an animal around in Fallout 76 felt like an exercise in anxiety. You had to equip specific Charisma perk cards, hunt down random spawn events on the edges of the map, and then pray your newly pacified creature survived the long trek back to your C.A.M.P.—only for a wandering super mutant or a stray glitch to wipe it out later. The upcoming World Pets update completely rewrites how animal allies function in Appalachia. Instead of being locked to your campsite as glorified lawn ornaments, these creatures are genuine wandering companions designed to follow you across the map. According to Bethesda's overview, you'll be able to choose your preferred companion, assign them a personalized name, and adjust their cosmetic appearance before heading out together. Having a loyal companion at your side finally brings that classic Dogmeat feel back to the survival loop, turning solo exploration into a genuine partnership.

From House Cats to Deathclaws

The creature roster showcased in the overview covers a surprisingly broad spectrum of Appalachian wildlife. If you lean toward traditional companion vibes, you have faithful dogs and house cats ready to join your journey. It's easy to picture plenty of players rolling through the Ash Heap with a resilient hound at their heel, or keeping a cat around while scavenging run-down homesteads. But Bethesda didn't stop at domestic friends. Players who want serious wasteland muscle can bring along fearsome Radhogs or even a massive, full-sized Deathclaw. Having a hulking apex predator stomping along behind you down the streets of Watoga or through the Cranberry Bog makes a massive statement, giving players who want a truly menacing aesthetic exactly what they've been hoping for since the game released.

Tactical Combat Commands: Attack, Defend, and Follow

One of the most important takeaways from the overview trailer is that these pets aren't merely passive vanity followers wandering aimlessly in the background. Bethesda has built out a direct tactical command system that gives you real-time control during firefights. Players will be able to actively issue commands to their companion, directing them to attack targeted enemies, hold ground and defend a specific position, or fall back into follow mode when things get too hairy. In high-density encounters like public events or tight interior dungeon crawls, having the ability to tell your Deathclaw to tie up a heavy mob while you reload your heavy guns or line up critical shots in V.A.T.S. adds a welcome layer of tactical control that Fallout 76 combat has largely lacked.

Leveling and Perk Synergy for Your Build

Progression is what will ultimately make or break this system for high-level players, and Bethesda is treating pets like full-fledged gameplay allies rather than temporary gimmicks. As you travel and fight alongside your companion, they gain experience and level up, turning into significantly stronger combat partners over time. Even better, leveling your pet unlocks unique companion perks designed to complement how you build your character. Whether you run a stealth sniper that needs a pet capable of distracting flanking ghouls, a melee brawler looking for supplementary crowd control, or a bloody build that benefits from defensive utility, these perk unlocks allow your pet to mesh mechanically with your gear and playstyle rather than just existing alongside it.

Why fans are talking about this: World Pets isn't just a cosmetic addition; it brings actual combat commands and build progression to animal companions in Fallout 76.

With the World Pets update dropping on December 1, Bethesda is delivering on one of the longest-standing requests from the Fallout 76 community. Having active companion commands, leveling trees, and build-defining perks brings a brand-new dynamic to both casual wandering and endgame farming runs. It's going to be wild seeing public events flooded with dozens of players flanked by cats, dogs, radhogs, and deathclaws all trading blows with wasteland bosses. We will be testing how the commands handle chaotic public events and tracking the best perk synergies the moment the update goes live.
