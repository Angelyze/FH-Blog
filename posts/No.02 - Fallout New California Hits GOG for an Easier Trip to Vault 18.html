Fallout: New California, the massive prequel mod for New Vegas, is now on GOG. Here is how to play this classic story-driven campaign easily today.

Radian-Helix Media's massive New Vegas prequel mod gets an official GOG release, making it easier than ever to skip manual mod managers.

If you have had the massive Fallout: New Vegas prequel mod Fallout: New California sitting on your backlog for years, playing it just got a whole lot simpler. As first reported by Rock Paper Shotgun, the DLC-sized project from Radian-Helix Media has officially launched on GOG. This release gives us a seamless way to experience one of the community's most ambitious story-driven campaigns without wrestling with manual mod managers or worrying about load orders. It is the perfect excuse to finally clear that item off your "to-play" list before the heat death of the universe.

What is Fallout: New California?

For those who missed the hype when it originally launched back in 2018, New California is a massive fan-made prequel set a couple of decades before the Courier gets shot in the head. You start your journey as an orphaned star athlete in Vault 18 on the night of the big 2260 Vault Ball championship. Naturally, things go sideways fast, forcing you out into the harsh California wastes. From there, your choices shape the future of the New California Republic long before the events of New Vegas even begin. It captures that specific, gritty tone of the early Fallout games while utilizing the robust engine that made New Vegas the gold standard for RPG fans.

Familiar Factions and Classic Lore

Radian-Helix Media didn't skimp on the narrative depth here. As you wander the wasteland, you will cross paths with a massive roster of classic Fallout factions. We are talking about the NCR under President Wendell Peterson, the Bishop crime family of New Reno, remnant Shi Clans, Enclave squads in power armor, and even Super Mutant armies. It is a dense, choice-heavy experience that feels like a proper bridge between the classic isometric games and Bethesda's modern era. It’s not just a combat slog; it’s a dialogue-heavy, consequence-driven adventure that rewards players who actually pay attention to the lore.

How to Play It on GOG

The biggest draw of this new GOG release is convenience. However, there is a catch: to use this specific version, you need to own the Fallout: New Vegas Ultimate Edition on GOG. This ensures the mod has the necessary assets to run smoothly. If you prefer to play on Steam, or if you’ve already got a heavily customized build that you aren't ready to abandon, don't worry. The developers are keeping the original download pages active on Nexus Mods and ModDB, so you can still mod your game the old-fashioned way if you prefer to keep your current setup intact.

Why It Still Holds Up

When reviewing the mod back in 2019, Dominic Tarason called it a 'well-portioned, highly replayable story' that serves as a must-play for fans who want a traditional single-player RPG. While some areas of the map can feel a bit empty compared to the official DLCs, the sheer scale of the branching paths and fully voiced dialogue makes it an incredible achievement. If you want a break from the multiplayer loop of West Virginia, this ten-hour campaign is the perfect excuse to return to the Mojave engine. It’s a testament to what a dedicated team can build when they have a clear vision for the wasteland.
