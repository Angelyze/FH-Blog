The Pint-Sized Slasher is coming to Fallout 76 on September 15 in the Slasher Update, alongside Season 26: Beware the Blood Moon. Here is what is new.

Bethesda has officially announced that the classic pre-war terror is returning to Appalachia on September 15.

We have official confirmation from Bethesda via Steam that a classic pre-war nightmare is coming to Fallout 76. On September 15, the Slasher Update will drop for all players, bringing a wave of terror inspired by the Capital Wasteland's most infamous pint-sized legend. Whether you are hunting down copycats or investigating emergency broadcasts, Appalachia is about to get a lot more dangerous.

Key facts

  • Confirmed via official Bethesda communications; coverage from Steam — New Vegas.
  • The Slasher Update officially launches on September 15 for all Fallout 76 players.
  • Features a storyline investigating a string of grisly crimes resembling the Pre-War Pint-Sized Slasher.
  • Players can clear Infestations of Slasher-worshipping cultists, tackle new Daily Ops, and dig up graves.
  • Launches Season 26: Beware the Blood Moon, featuring creepy-themed rewards like the Blood Moon Weather Control Station.

The Return of a Pre-War Legend

If you spent any time exploring the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3, you probably remember the Pint-Sized Slasher. That creepy clown mask and kitchen knife combo is burned into the collective memory of the franchise. Now, Bethesda Creative Director Jon Rush and Senior Community Manager Richard Stanford have pulled back the curtain on a massive new update dedicated entirely to this pint-sized terror. Arriving on September 15, the update introduces a string of grisly crimes across Appalachia that look suspiciously like the work of the pre-war serial killer. It is up to us to figure out if the legend has somehow returned or if we are dealing with a dangerous new wave of copycats.

Investigating the Bloody Wake

This is not just a passive lore drop; players will be actively hunting for clues. Bethesda is rolling out "Breaking News" emergency broadcasts over multiple weeks to update us on the latest leads in the investigation. The gameplay loop sounds delightfully eerie. We will be clearing out Infestations overrun by Slasher-worshipping cultists, taking on brand-new Daily Ops challenges, and even digging up graves to uncover the truth. It is a great excuse to get back out into the forest and do some actual detective work, even if it means looking over our shoulders a bit more than usual.

Season 26: Beware the Blood Moon

Alongside the narrative update, September 15 marks the debut of Season 26, appropriately titled "Beware the Blood Moon." As we earn S.C.O.R.E. and collect Tickets, we will be able to redeem them for some highly anticipated, creepy-themed cosmetic and camp items. Bethesda teased several rewards, including the Batty Skirt, the Blood Moon Power Armor Paint, the Creepy Cultist Well, and a brand-new Blood Moon Weather Control Station to completely change the vibe of your C.A.M.P. For those who want to dig even deeper, Legacy Seasons will also offer additional rewards to chase.

What We Are Still Waiting to See

While the release date and the core activities are locked in, there are still a few details Bethesda is keeping under wraps. We do not yet know the full list of rewards for Season 26, or exactly how many weeks the "Breaking News" broadcasts will run. However, having a concrete date like September 15 gives us a clear target to prepare our builds. Whether you are a classic Fallout fan who loves the nod to Fallout 3's Tranquility Lane or a 76 regular looking for fresh Daily Ops, this update is shaping up to be a perfect transition into the autumn season.

Why fans are talking about this: Bringing back the Pint-Sized Slasher is a brilliant nod to classic Fallout lore that gives Appalachia the exact kind of eerie, detective-driven mystery the community loves.

While we still have a few weeks to wait before the emergency broadcasts start airing, the September 15 release date is locked in. Make sure your gear is repaired and your C.A.M.P. defenses are set before the blood moon rises. We will be tracking every new lead over at the Fallout Hub Discord as soon as the update drops.
