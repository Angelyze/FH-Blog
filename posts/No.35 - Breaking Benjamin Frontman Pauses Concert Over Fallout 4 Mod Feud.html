Breaking Benjamin singer Benjamin Burnley paused a New Jersey concert to rant about a Fallout 4 modding feud and plug his 'True First Person Mod'.

Editorial note: This article is based on press reporting and has not been confirmed by the developer or publisher.

According to reports, rock star Benjamin Burnley used his band's stage to call out a rival modder and rally fans to download his Fallout 4 project.

I’ve seen some weird stuff in the Fallout community over the years, but a multi-platinum rock star using his stage time to settle a beef over camera scripts is a new one for me. If you had 'rock star pauses concert to air out Fallout 4 modding drama' on your bingo card, congratulations. As first reported by IGN, Breaking Benjamin frontman Benjamin Burnley took a five-minute detour during a recent show in New Jersey to put a rival modder on blast. While Bethesda hasn't weighed in on this bizarre crossover, the footage circulating online is as real as it gets.

Key facts

  • Reported by IGN; not yet confirmed by the developer or publisher.
  • Reported by IGN; the incident occurred during a concert on Sunday, September 6.
  • Breaking Benjamin frontman Benjamin Burnley revealed he is an 'amateur modder' for Skyrim and Fallout 4.
  • Burnley stopped a concert in Holmdel, New Jersey, to rant about a feud over his 'True First Person Mod'.

A Rock Star's Grievance on the Big Screen

According to the IGN report, Burnley stopped his band's performance in Holmdel, New Jersey, to address a very specific, very personal grievance. For nearly five minutes, the singer explained to a crowd of rock fans that he spends his free time as an amateur modder for Skyrim and Fallout 4. This wasn't just a passing comment, either; the venue's massive production screens actually flashed his Nexus Mods username, 'killemloud', and the name of his project: 'True First Person Mod'. It is the kind of surreal community crossover you couldn't make up if you tried, seeing a professional lighting rig used to promote a camera tweak.

What is the 'True First Person Mod'?

The mod itself is designed to address a common player preference regarding immersion. As Burnley explained on stage, the mod ensures that when you play in first-person, you stay in first-person. This allows you to see your character's body and interact with the workshop area without the camera forcing a jarring perspective shift, all while keeping third-person functionality intact for when you want to see your armor. It is a solid quality-of-life tweak that many of us usually look for on Nexus, but according to Burnley, another member of the modding community didn't share his vision, sparking a bitter online feud that eventually spilled onto a physical concert stage.

The Feud Goes Public

The IGN report details how Burnley used his platform to rally his audience against his rival. He claimed a 'clique' in the modding community had been bullying him, calling his mod trash and trying to block him from updating it. 'I'm doing this every fucking night, and blowing his fucking downloads out of the water,' Burnley told the crowd, pleading with them to help him 'destroy this fucking jerk.' He expressed frustration that the other modder was 'shitting' on his work right off the bat and preventing him from learning and growing the project. While most of the crowd found the rant hilarious, a few vocal concertgoers reportedly yelled at the frontman to stop talking about Update 1.2 and just play some music.

The Internet Reacts to 'killemloud'

Unsurprisingly, the footage—originally captured and posted to Reddit by user 'im_all_ears'—has gone viral. The Fallout community is largely delighted by the revelation that the voice behind massive mid-2000s rock hits is spending his nights tweaking Bethesda camera scripts. It’s worth remembering that Breaking Benjamin has a history with gaming, famously featuring in the Halo 2 soundtrack, but seeing the frontman get into the weeds of Nexus Mods drama is a different level of fandom. While we wait to see if this massive publicity stunt actually breaks the download charts, it is a funny reminder of how deep the love for Fallout 4 runs, even when you're touring the country.

Why fans are talking about this: Sometimes the wildest drama in the Commonwealth isn't between the Brotherhood and the Institute, but on a concert stage in New Jersey over a camera script.

We will be keeping an eye on the Nexus download counts to see if the Breaking Benjamin army actually manages to blow his rival's numbers out of the water.
