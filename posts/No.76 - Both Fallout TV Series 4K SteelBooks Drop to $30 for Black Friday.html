Grab Prime Video's Fallout Season 1 and Season 2 4K SteelBooks for just $30 each during Amazon's Black Friday sale, as reported by IGN.

Editorial note: This article is based on press reporting and has not been confirmed by the developer or publisher.

Amazon has slashed prices on Prime Video's Season 1 and Season 2 physical releases, according to a new report from IGN.

If you've been holding out on adding Prime Video's Fallout show to your physical media shelf, today is the day to pull the trigger. IGN reports that Amazon's Black Friday deals have officially kicked off, knocking the 4K SteelBooks for both seasons down to $29.99 each. That is a pretty significant drop from the original $43 sticker price. While Bethesda hasn't been shouting about this from the rooftops yet, the listings are live and ready for those of us who prefer owning our discs rather than just renting them from a streaming subscription that could change its mind at any moment.

Key facts

  • Reported by IGN; not yet confirmed by the developer or publisher.
  • Reported by IGN; deals are live as part of Amazon's official Black Friday event.
  • Both Season 1 and Season 2 4K SteelBooks are discounted from $43 to $29.99 each.
  • Season 1 includes 12 featurettes like 'Console to Camera' and 'Becoming the Ghoul,' plus mini character posters.
  • Season 2 features New Vegas-themed artwork, matching spine art, and exclusive extras like the RobCo Animated Series.
  • Season 3 is currently in development with a predicted release window of Spring 2027.

Season 1 SteelBook Hits Its Lowest Price

According to IGN, the Season 1 4K SteelBook has finally hit that $30 sweet spot. For those who haven't seen the physical packaging yet, it's a three-disc set wrapped in a metal case featuring the iconic Power Armor helmet. The spine uses that traditional Fallout font we all know, which is a nice touch for the shelf. Inside, you're getting more than just the episodes; there's a hefty list of featurettes like 'Console to Camera,' 'Becoming the Ghoul,' and 'Set Your Sets on 2296.' It even includes 'Inside Seasons One Prosthetics & Makeup,' which is worth a watch just to see the work that went into making the Ghouls look appropriately crispy without looking like cheap Halloween masks.

New Vegas Vibes for the Season 2 Set

The Season 2 SteelBook is also seeing the exact same discount down to $29.99. This one is particularly cool for the New Vegas fans among us, as the cover art shows Lucy, Maximus, and The Ghoul trekking toward that unmistakable, ominous skyline. IGN notes that the spine is designed to match the first season perfectly, so they'll look uniform when lined up. This set also packs in three discs and some unique extras you won't find on Prime Video, including a 'Fake Talk Show' segment and the 'RobCo Animated Series' featurette. You also get cast commentary with Kyle MacLachlan and Aaron Moten, which usually offers some of the best 'behind-the-curtain' stories from the set.

The Long Road to Season 3

With Season 2 having wrapped up its run earlier this year, the focus is slowly shifting toward what's next. The IGN report reminds us that while we're waiting, Todd Howard has already started teasing Season 3. He mentioned they are planning to explore territory 'never seen before in the games,' which is a bold claim given how much ground the franchise has covered over the last 25 years. We don't have a hard release date yet, but the current educated guess is Spring 2027. Grabbing these physical copies now is a good way to kill the time and ensure you have the highest possible bit-rate version of the show while we wait for the production cycle to churn along.

Why Physical Media Still Matters for Fallout

I know most of us already watched these the night they dropped on Prime, but $30 for a 4K SteelBook is a solid deal for the collectors. Streaming is convenient, but it doesn't beat the uncompressed 4K video and audio quality you get from a physical disc—especially in a show with this much visual detail in the set design. Plus, with the way digital licenses have been disappearing lately, actually owning the 'RobCo Animated Series' and the 'Making of' featurettes feels like a safer bet. If you're a fan of the show's aesthetic, the miniature character posters included inside the cases are a nice little bonus for the desk or the wall.

Why fans are talking about this: At $30 each, these 4K SteelBooks offer the best visual quality and a deep dive into the show's production for a much more reasonable price than their $43 launch tag.

It's worth noting that Black Friday stock can be a bit of a gamble, especially for limited edition SteelBooks. If you're planning on completing the set, I'd suggest checking the listings sooner rather than later. Once these sell out, they usually end up on eBay for double the price.
