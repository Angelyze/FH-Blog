Looking at your Paper Map will show the general area that one can be found, but it will be up to you to find its exact location.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p class=&quot;bb_paragraph&quot;&gt;If you want to hunt for Infestations, we recommend you create or join an Exploration Public Team because on an active server full of hunters, you might find them difficult to find.
